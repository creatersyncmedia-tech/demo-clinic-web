About Us

A Dental Clinic Built Around Your Family

SmileCare Dental Clinic was started in 2014 by Dr. Ananya Kapoor with a simple goal — make dental visits something families in Jaipur don't dread. What began as a small two-chair clinic in Malviya Nagar has grown into a full-service practice, but the approach has stayed the same: unhurried appointments and treatment plans that are explained in plain language before any work begins.

Whether you're bringing in a nervous first-time patient or coming in for your yearly cleaning, our team adjusts to your pace. We use modern, well-maintained equipment and follow strict sterilisation protocols, so you can focus on your treatment and not worry about the rest.
